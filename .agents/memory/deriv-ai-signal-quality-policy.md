Show a generated Over/Under, Even/Odd, or Matches setup only when every condition defined for that setup passes and the live feed is fresh. Hide incomplete candidates rather than listing their failed conditions. A qualification label means the rule checks passed; it is not a claim of guaranteed profit.

**Why:** The user clarified that only qualified setups should appear on the signal boards; all other candidates must stay hidden.

**How to apply:** Keep visibility and trade handoff qualification consistent across all three signal boards. Preserve each strategy's existing entry-timing semantics; a timing reference is not automatically a mandatory condition.
