---
name: AI Signals qualification policy
description: User requirements for strong and weak setup labels across AI Signals markets.
---

Only describe a generated Over/Under, Even/Odd, or Matches setup as strong when every condition defined for that setup passes and the live feed is fresh. Keep incomplete candidates visible with the specific failed conditions, but do not allow their trade action. A strong label means the rule checks passed; it is not a claim of guaranteed profit.

**Why:** The user asked that these signal types avoid weak or guessed entries, expose weak conditions, and clearly identify strong setups.

**How to apply:** Keep qualification consistent across all three signal boards and their handoff actions. Preserve each strategy's existing entry-timing semantics; a timing reference is not automatically a mandatory condition.
