Binary Matrix qualifies each existing rule from its own historical evidence, then locks the selected market and rule before the latest Last-X pattern appears. It waits for that exact configured pattern, checks live payout after the pattern and indicator conditions appear, and buys only on a later fresh matching tick. Over/Under also need current MACD (12/26/9) and RSI(14) alignment; Even/Odd remains governed only by digit rules.

**Why:** The user approved MACD and RSI as directional filters for Over/Under while preserving the existing digit rules and excluding ROC.

**How to apply:** Keep historical and live rules aligned. Once selected, do not switch the locked market or contract direction while waiting for its Last-X pattern. Check payout only after the Last-X pattern and current Over/Under indicators align; then require a later fresh tick before buying. Do not apply price direction to parity contracts or add candle-color conditions unless the Binary Matrix strategy itself introduces them.
