---
name: Alpha Scan research gate
description: The safety boundary for the Alpha Scan AI product surface
---

Alpha Scan AI's first phase is an evidence layer: dynamically discovered public Synthetic Index metadata, tick-history snapshots, descriptive features, and regime labels. It must stay paper-only and display `SKIP — validation pending` until contract-specific labels, chronological splits, walk-forward testing, calibration, realistic backtesting, and risk gates are implemented.

**Why:** The product brief explicitly rejects fabricated probabilities, guaranteed profitability, indicator-only claims, and real-money execution before statistically validated out-of-sample evidence exists.

**How to apply:** Add research and observability before adding trade actions. Never turn directional imbalance, volatility, or regime heuristics into an ML probability or execution recommendation.
