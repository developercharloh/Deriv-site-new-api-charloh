Out-of-sample direction check

A chronological replay tested whether the weighted recent-digit chooser picked a better next-tick direction than a fixed Over 4 baseline. The result below describes this sample only.

Weighted chooser 47.05% 9,175 wins from 19,500 next-tick checks
Fixed Over 4 baseline 49.97% 9,745 wins from the same checks
Difference vs baseline −2.93 pp 95% paired block-bootstrap interval: −3.48 to −2.33 pp
The chooser underperformed the baseline in this holdout. It also had a lower hit rate than fixed Over 4 on each of the 13 tested markets. This does not establish how the bot will perform in future ticks.

For the Smart Over 2 template’s default market, 1HZ50V, the chooser won on 46.27% of holdout checks versus 49.33% for fixed Over 4. The longest losing run across the market samples ranged from 8 to 14; digit 4 occurred 1,973 times and was counted as a loss for both contracts.

How the replay was run

  • Retrieved 5,000 public historical ticks for each of 13 Deriv volatility indices. For each market, the earliest 70% was kept as the training period and the most recent 30% as the holdout.
  • At each holdout tick, the existing rule analyzed only the preceding 100 digits, using its 10-, 30-, and 100-tick windows weighted 2, 1.5, and 1, then selected Over 4 or Under 4 for the next digit.
  • A digit above 4 counted as an Over 4 win; a digit below 4 counted as an Under 4 win; digit 4 was a loss for either choice. The training-only majority direction selected Over 4 on all 13 markets.
  • The paired difference interval used 2,000 moving-block bootstrap resamples with 100-tick blocks to retain some short-range dependence between neighboring predictions.

Important limits

This is a rolling one-tick classification replay, not a simulation of the complete Recovery 1–3 sequence. It does not include broker payouts, stake progression, contract costs, or session risk limits. A higher hit rate would not by itself demonstrate profitability; this sample does not show that the weighted chooser has a predictive advantage.
