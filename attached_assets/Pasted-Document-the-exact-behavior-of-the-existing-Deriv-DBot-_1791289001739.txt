SIDE SWITCHING
- “Switch After” controls how many REAL contracts settle before the bot changes from the current side to the opposite side.
- With the default value of 3:
  - Start with Even.
  - After 3 settled real contracts, switch to Odd.
  - After 3 settled real contracts on Odd, switch back to Even.

- After switching sides, the real-trade counter resets to zero.
- The counter advances ONLY after real contracts settle.
- Virtual contracts do NOT advance the real-trade counter.
- The side switch does not mean the bot has predicted that the next digit will be Even or Odd.

EVEN/ODD VIRTUAL HOOK
- The Virtual Hook applies to the Even/Odd strategy.
- Before allowing real contracts for the active side, the bot simulates one-tick Even/Odd outcomes using incoming market ticks.
- These are VIRTUAL observations only. They are not broker purchases.
- Virtual observations:
  - Do not use the real stake.
  - Do not change the Martingale stake.
  - Do not count as real trades.
  - Do not increase or decrease the real-trade counter.
  - Do not directly trigger the total-profit stop conditions.
- A virtual Even wins when the observed last digit is even.
- A virtual Odd wins when the observed last digit is odd.
- A virtual loss increments the consecutive virtual-loss count for the active side.
- A virtual win resets the consecutive virtual-loss count for that side to zero.
- The bot allows real contracts for the active side only after the configured number of consecutive virtual losses has been reached.
- With the default threshold, the active side must experience three consecutive virtual losses before it qualifies for real trading.
- After the side changes from Even to Odd, or Odd to Even, the new side must qualify separately.
- The virtual-loss count starts over for the new side.
- Once the active side passes the virtual-loss threshold, the bot can place real contracts for that side until the normal “Switch After” rule causes the A/B switch.
- Virtual activity must be treated and logged as virtual events, not as real trades.
